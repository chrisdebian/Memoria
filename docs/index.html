Search your photos with words.

Memoria is a free Android app that finds photos in your gallery by what's in them. No cloud, no account, nothing leaves your phone.

Download for Android View source

Free and open source. Android 9 or newer. About 100 MB.

See it in action

Swipe through the app.

What it does

Everything runs on your phone.

Five languages

Search in English, Arabic, French, Russian or Spanish. No translation step.

Search by meaning

Type "birthday cake" or "snowy mountains". Photos don't need tags or captions.

Search with a photo

Pick any image, crop it to one face or object, or share one from another app into Memoria.

Find Similar

Open a photo and tap once to see more like it.

Smart Albums

Experimental. Create an album and Memoria suggests matching photos as your library grows.

Filters and folders

Narrow results by date, folder or camera. Choose which folders get indexed, so screenshots stay out.

Private by design

  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads.
  • Your photo index and search history stay on your device and are excluded from system backups.
  • The code is open, so you can check all of this yourself.

How it works

Memoria runs Apple's MobileCLIP model on your phone. It turns each photo, and each thing you type, into a list of numbers. Photos whose numbers are closest to your search come first. Indexing runs in the background, and you can pause it any time.
